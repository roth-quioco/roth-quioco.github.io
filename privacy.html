Privacy Policy

Daily Trends Uploader · Last updated: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Trends Uploader ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Daily Trends YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to YouTube. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2. Information we access

With your permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, the app requests one OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It uses this scope only to:

  • upload video files to the authorised user's own YouTube channel, and
  • set each video's title, description, tags, category, audience ("not made for kids") and scheduled publish time.

The app does not access, collect or store any other YouTube or Google account data. That includes channel data, viewer data, comments, analytics, contacts or email.

3. How information is used

The OAuth access token and refresh token issued by Google are used only to make the upload requests described above.

4. How information is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a local file. The app has no servers or databases of its own and no analytics. It does not send your information anywhere other than Google's own API endpoints.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose any user information to third parties.

6. Cookies and tracking

The app does not use cookies or tracking technologies. This website stores no cookies of its own. The embedded YouTube player on the home page is provided by YouTube and is subject to Google's policies.

7. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept only while the app is in use. They are deleted from the local computer when access is revoked or when the app is removed.
  • If the app stops being able to refresh its authorisation, stored tokens are deleted and no API data is kept.
  • Stored API data is refreshed or deleted in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies, and is never kept for more than 30 days after it is no longer needed.
  • To request deletion of any data related to your use of the app, email rothquioco@gmail.com. We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: rothquioco@gmail.com
