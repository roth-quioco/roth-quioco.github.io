Daily Trends Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Daily Trends Uploader ("the app") is a private tool. It is used only by the owner of the Daily Trends YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube API Services, with one permission: youtube.upload. It uses that permission only to upload videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to the channel owner's own channel, and to set their publish time. It does not read, collect or store any other YouTube or Google account data, viewer data, comments or analytics.

What is stored, and where

The OAuth access token Google issues is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's. The app has no servers, databases or analytics of its own. It does not use cookies.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps & services. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes the app's access.

YouTube and Google policies

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
